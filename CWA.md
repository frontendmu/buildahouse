## CWA - Domestic New Supply

Documents needed:

- **Title Deed** — copy **plus the original**, which is returned after verification
- Copy of National ID Card of applicant/Proxy/Landlord/Owner/Heirs
- 2 Copies of Site/Location plan
- Building and Land Use Permit — see [[permit]]

If you are **not the owner**:
- Letter of Authorisation from the owner, plus the owner's ID card
- Tenants also need the rent book

### Fees

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Processing fee (non-refundable) | **Rs 500** |
| Installation fee | **Rs 2,200** over and above the processing fee |

⚠️ Budget **Rs 2,700**, not Rs 500 — the installation fee is charged in addition. Long connections from a distant main may cost more; confirm with CWA. See [[finance]].
