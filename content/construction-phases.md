# The Construction Phases

What actually happens on site, in order, and **what to check at each stage before it is covered up**. Concrete and plaster hide everything. Once a stage is buried, your only options are trust or demolition.

Your architect should inspect and certify each stage before you release payment — see [[contractor]].

## Before Work Starts

- [ ] BLP issued and the local authority informed that construction is starting — see [[permit]]
- [ ] Contract signed, with payment stages and variation procedure — see [[contractor]]
- [ ] Structural engineer's drawings on site
- [ ] Contractor's insurances confirmed — see [[finance]]
- [ ] Site water and electricity arranged, and it is clear who pays
- [ ] Neighbours informed
- [ ] Photograph the empty site and the boundary pegs

---

## 1. Site Setting Out and Excavation

The building is marked out on the ground and foundation trenches are dug.

**Check before proceeding:**
- [ ] The house position matches the approved plan, including **setback distances from every boundary** — a house built too close to a boundary is a permit breach and very expensive to resolve
- [ ] Orientation is as designed
- [ ] Excavation depth matches the engineer's drawings
- [ ] Trenches are on firm ground, not loose fill
- [ ] Levels correct — including any raising of the house for flood risk, per [[architect]]

## 2. Foundation

Footings, reinforcement, and concrete.

**Check before the concrete pour — this is your last chance:**
- [ ] Steel **diameter, spacing, and number of bars** match the engineer's drawings
- [ ] Correct **concrete cover** to the steel (spacers/chairs in place, steel not sitting on the soil)
- [ ] Reinforcement is clean, tied, and stable
- [ ] Correct **concrete grade** ordered
- [ ] **Photograph the reinforcement thoroughly from several angles**
- [ ] Curing plan agreed — concrete must be kept wet, not left to dry in the sun

## 3. Columns, Ground Beams, and Backfilling

**Check:**
- [ ] Column positions and sizes match the drawings
- [ ] Starter bars and lap lengths as specified
- [ ] Formwork straight, plumb, and properly braced
- [ ] Any plumbing or conduit passing through the foundation is placed **before** backfilling
- [ ] Backfill compacted in layers, not dumped
- [ ] Anti-termite treatment applied if specified
- [ ] Damp proof membrane laid where specified

## 4. Walls / Blockwork

**Check:**
- [ ] Block type and size as specified
- [ ] Walls plumb and courses level
- [ ] **Openings for doors and windows in the right places and the right sizes** — measure them against the plan now
- [ ] Lintels over openings as specified
- [ ] Room dimensions measured against the plan before plastering

## 5. Roof Slab Casting

The biggest single pour of the project, and a milestone with real consequences — the roof slab grant in [[mra]] relates to this stage.

**Check before the pour:**
- [ ] Formwork and props adequate and properly supported — slab formwork failure during a pour is a serious safety event
- [ ] Steel layout, spacing, top and bottom reinforcement, and cover verified against the engineer's drawings
- [ ] **All conduits, sleeves, and openings placed before the pour** — for plumbing stacks, electrical drops, and any future stair or opening
- [ ] Concrete grade confirmed
- [ ] Pour planned to complete in one continuous operation
- [ ] **Photograph everything before the pour**
- [ ] Curing arranged for the days after — kept wet, and props left in place for the period the engineer specifies
- [ ] Slope and outlets for **roof drainage** as designed — a flat slab that ponds water will leak eventually
- [ ] Waterproofing specification confirmed and included in the contract

⚠️ **Do not let props be removed early to speed the next stage.** The concrete has not reached strength.

## 6. Plumbing and Electrical Rough-In

Pipes and conduits are chased into walls and slabs before plastering.

**Check before plastering closes it all in:**
- [ ] Plumbing pressure-tested for leaks
- [ ] Drainage falls correct, and connected to the septic tank or sewer per [[wma]]
- [ ] **Socket, switch, and light positions match the agreed electrical layout** — walk the house room by room and confirm each one at its actual height and position. Changes are free now and expensive later.
- [ ] Provision for water heater, air conditioning units, and outdoor points
- [ ] Data and TV conduits run, kept separate from power — see [[telecom]]
- [ ] Provision for future solar panels if planned — see [[architect]]
- [ ] Earthing arrangement in place, as CEB will require
- [ ] **Photograph every wall and ceiling before plastering**, with something in shot for scale

## 7. Plaster, Screed, and Waterproofing

- [ ] Plaster thickness and finish as specified
- [ ] Screed falls correct in bathrooms, kitchen, laundry, and terraces — water must run to the drain
- [ ] **Wet areas waterproofed and, ideally, water-tested before tiling**
- [ ] Roof waterproofing applied as specified and tested
- [ ] Walls checked for straightness before tiling begins

## 8. Finishes

Tiles, doors, windows, joinery, sanitary ware, painting, kitchen.

- [ ] Materials match what was specified and approved — check deliveries against the specification, not just the invoice
- [ ] Tile layout agreed before laying starts
- [ ] Doors and windows fitted square, sealed, and operating properly
- [ ] Window and door frames sealed against water ingress
- [ ] Sanitary ware installed and tested
- [ ] Electrical fittings installed and every circuit tested
- [ ] Painting: correct preparation and number of coats

## 9. Externals

Frequently excluded from the main contract — confirm with your contractor.

- [ ] Water tank and pump installed and working
- [ ] Boundary wall and gate
- [ ] Driveway and paths, with drainage away from the house
- [ ] Ground levels sloping **away** from the building
- [ ] Landscaping

## 10. Snagging and Handover

Before the final payment, walk the house with your architect and write a **snag list**.

- [ ] Every room inspected in daylight: walls, ceilings, floors, paint
- [ ] Every door and window opened, closed, and locked
- [ ] Every tap, shower, and WC run and checked for leaks
- [ ] Every socket, switch, and light tested
- [ ] Drainage checked by running water
- [ ] Tiles checked for hollow sounds and lippage
- [ ] Snag list agreed in writing with dates for completion
- [ ] **Retention held back until snags are cleared and the defects liability period ends** — see [[contractor]]
- [ ] As-built drawings, warranties, and manuals collected
- [ ] All keys handed over

Then proceed to [[occupation-certificate]].

## ⚠️ Important Note

🚨 **The single most valuable habit on site: photograph every stage before it is covered.** Reinforcement before concrete, conduits before plaster, foundations before backfill. It costs nothing, and it is the only evidence you will ever have.
